BareSQL.com would like to hear from anyone who tries BareSQL, about what it found and what it missed.

Bug reports help most: an audit line the server disagrees with, a lint message that's wrong for your engine, two answers that compare called equal and aren't, or anything else that looks off. Ideas are welcome too, for a check BareSQL doesn't do yet or a SQL difference it doesn't know.

Write to **[info@baresql.com](mailto:info@baresql.com)**.

Bug reports are also welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/softdevstudio/baresql/issues). It helps to include what `baresql version` prints and the smallest SQL that shows the problem, with the engine and its version.

Please don't send real reports from production databases. Even without sampled values, a report names your tables, columns and logins. A few made-up tables that show the same thing are enough.

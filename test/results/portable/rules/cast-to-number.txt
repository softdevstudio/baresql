cast-to-number (error): CAST to an integer or a decimal

CAST(5.7 AS INTEGER) is 5 on SQLite, 6 on PostgreSQL and MariaDB, and an error
on MySQL, which wants SIGNED.

SQLite drops the fraction and PostgreSQL and MariaDB round it. MySQL takes
only SIGNED and UNSIGNED as integer types in CAST, and PostgreSQL refuses
both. Decimals differ as well: SQLite ignores the scale of CAST(x AS
DECIMAL(10, 1)) and keeps every digit, and MySQL and MariaDB read DECIMAL
without a precision as DECIMAL(10, 0), a whole number.

Example:
    SELECT CAST(5.7 AS INTEGER)

    SQLite 3.45.1        5
    PostgreSQL 16.15     6
    MySQL 8.0.46         error: You have an error in your SQL syntax; check the manual that corresponds to your MySQL server version for the r...
    MariaDB 10.11.14     6

The same on every engine:
    SELECT ROUND(5.7)

    SQLite 3.45.1        6.0
    PostgreSQL 16.15     6
    MySQL 8.0.46         6
    MariaDB 10.11.14     6

Round on purpose with ROUND(x) or ROUND(x, digits), which every engine has.

              SQLite PostgreSQL MySQL MariaDB
INTEGER       5      6          no    6
INT           5      6          no    6
BIGINT        yes    yes        no    no
SMALLINT      yes    yes        no    no
TINYINT       yes    no         no    no
SIGNED        5.7    no         6     6
UNSIGNED      5.7    no         6     6
NUMERIC       yes    yes        no    no
DECIMAL       5.75   5.75       6     6
DECIMAL(p, s) 5.75   5.8        5.8   5.8
